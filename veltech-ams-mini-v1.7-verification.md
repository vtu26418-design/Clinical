Closing or filtering a viewer/card does not lose pending reconciliation. Failed comments recover drafts; replies stay threaded and inline. ## Delivery and operating limits

The ZIP excludes runtime/private state, credentials, cookies, uploaded media, caches and real student records. The public preview contains fictional data and disables private sign-in/payments. The source project includes Windows PowerShell scripts, Docker services and Atlas setup. It is an installable web/PWA project, not an APK.

Campus browser sessions are rolling30-day sessions, revocable at logout. Upstream AMS clients are process-local, limited to100 owners and30minutes, and need a fresh sign-in after restart/expiry. Keep one academic worker; horizontal upstream-session sharing is not implemented. Multiple campus replicas on one Docker host share Mongo sessions/rate limits and media. This is not a measured production capacity guarantee.

AMS can require manual verification. Private files, camera/GPS, physical presence, Internet email/SMS, real Atlas/Windows Desktop, merchant capture/refunds and production load/failover require operator checks. Database and media free quotas are finite. Unknown source layouts remain unavailable instead of fabricated. See README.md, deployment.md and storage.md for setup and limits.
