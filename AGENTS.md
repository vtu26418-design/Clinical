# CTI Engineering Rules

1. Never fabricate clinical-trial data or research metrics.
2. Never commit secrets.
3. Preserve raw AACT files unchanged.
4. Prevent temporal/data leakage.
5. Keep PCI methodology explicit.
6. Keep structured-only, text-only and multimodal experiments comparable.
7. SHAP is attribution evidence, not causal proof.
8. Sample data is for software smoke tests only.
9. Every final result must be reproducible from code/configuration.
10. Treat CTI as research decision support, not clinical or regulatory advice.
